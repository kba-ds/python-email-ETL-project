Faster verification. Fewer failed texts. One tap on WhatsApp. ⚡

Phone verification on Freelancer just got a more reliable path. Selected users can now receive their verification code (OTP) through WhatsApp instead of SMS, making the whole flow faster, more dependable, and easier to complete on the first try.

If you've ever stared at a "waiting for SMS" screen that never arrived, you know the problem this solves. Codes sent through WhatsApp travel over the internet instead of mobile networks, which means fewer delays, fewer failed deliveries, and a smoother run from start to finish.
