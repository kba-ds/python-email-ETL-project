Green means online. The rest tells you when. ⚡
Chat status on Freelancer just got more useful. The grey "offline" dot has been replaced with a last-active timestamp, so you can see how recently someone was on the platform (up to 24 hours back) before you decide whether to send that message.

This is for everyone who's hesitated to message a client or freelancer across time zones, second-guessed whether a quick question would land, or written off a "grey dot" only to find out later the person had been online twenty minutes ago. Now you have the context to act with confidence.

A small balance. A stronger bidding floor. A better marketplace for everyone. ⚡
There's now a Minimum Account Balance requirement before placing a bid on certain projects on Freelancer. A simple step to keep bids on these projects coming from freelancers who are seriously in the game.

Here's the short version: you'll need at least $20 USD (or the equivalent in your supported currency) available in your site wallet to bid on these projects. That balance can come from funds already in your wallet, deposits, or other credited amounts.
